# Round 1 independent review

Verdict: **REVISE**.

The reviewer judged the pivot substantially better than the earlier
route-score-as-mechanism framing, but not ready for a mediation claim.

## Fatal issues for readiness

1. The paired edge difference is only an algebraic contrast until world
   alignment, candidate orientation, edge matching, and lexical controls are
   formalized.
2. Bidirectional relay exchange is not automatically a natural/direct/indirect
   causal effect in a nonlinear transformer. The intervention object must be
   fixed precisely.
3. The currently implemented confirmation is a bounded value-channel witness;
   it does not cover full K/Q routing, MLP/residual mediation, multiple carriers,
   or long-path interference.
4. Controlled pairs alone cannot support a natural hallucination claim. A
   source-balanced, held-out bridge is required after the protocol is frozen.

## Required revisions

- Rename the candidate to a mediation *screen* until finite causal assumptions
  and channel coverage are established.
- Define worlds, shared prefix, candidate contrast, alignment map, edge match,
  intervention site/object, and no-path coverage.
- Make sequence log-odds match the actual evaluator.
- Freeze path selection before held-out evaluation and keep H/N labels out of it.
- Evaluate local-to-finite sign, rank, and magnitude calibration across doses.
- Include paraphrase, rename, irrelevant-source, equal-norm random,
  neighbor-carrier, direct-only, V-only, and K-only controls.

## Nearest prior work identified by the reviewer

Causal mediation, causal tracing/ROME, path patching, attribution patching,
activation-patching methodology, circuit faithfulness, AttnLRP, Lookbacks, and
attribution/circuit tracing. Novelty can only rest on the combined semantic
constraint pair, signed physical last-crossing partition, and finite witness
protocol after empirical validation.
