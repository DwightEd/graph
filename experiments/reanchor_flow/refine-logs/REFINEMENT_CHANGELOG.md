# Refinement changelog

## Initial diagnosis

- Separated conditional source allocation from native local tangent transport.
- Treated last-crossing closure as an accounting certificate, not causal proof.
- Rejected observed-versus-runner signs and old route scores as truth/mechanism
  definitions.

## Round 1

- Independent verdict: REVISE.
- Renamed the method from mediation to mediation screen.
- Formalized worlds, sequence outcome, alignment, edge matching, unmatched mass,
  intervention object, dose response, and failure coverage.
- Narrowed current confirmation to a bounded value-channel witness.

## Round 2

- Independent verdict: READY for experiment planning under the bounded-witness
  claim.
- Preserved explicit exclusions: no full mediation, natural indirect effect,
  hallucination mechanism, detector gain, or cross-generator causal claim.

## Code architecture influenced by the refinement

- Allocation and tangent operators now share only native captured operands.
- Artifact identity, portable atomic publication, and writer leases are isolated.
- Last-crossing math is separate from streamed edge persistence and closure
  publication.
- Outputs declare distinct method identities and share an offline evaluator.
