# Final refined proposal: Counterfactual Last-Crossing Mediation Screen

Status: **READY for experiment planning under a bounded-witness claim**. No new
8B result, detector gain, or hallucination mechanism is claimed.

## Narrow contribution

A label-free, externally anchored screening-and-witness protocol tests whether a
response-token carrier transports an evidence constraint. Aligned minimal-pair
constraint edits induce signed, physical-head last-crossing local effects;
preregistered carriers are then tested by bidirectional finite post-WO message
exchange over a dose grid, using evidence-consistent sequence log-odds.

With the current finite-confirmation implementation, the defensible claim is
narrower still: a **bounded value-channel causal witness** selected by a signed
last-crossing screen. Full mediation, natural indirect effects, K/Q/MLP/residual
coverage, hallucination mechanism, and detector performance are explicitly out
of scope until separately demonstrated.

## Formal contract

- W+ and W- differ in one declared entity, negation, temporal, or scope
  constraint.
- A frozen label-free alignment phi maps shared-prefix/candidate positions.
- Both candidate sequences are grammatical and swap their evidence-consistency
  orientation between worlds.
- pi(e) preserves layer, physical head, V/K branch, and aligned source/query
  positions; unmatched mass is reported.
- Each world must pass hop and last-crossing closure before pairing.
- The primary finite intervention is exactly the counterfactual post-WO
  physical-head message delta at one selected carrier. K-cache and V-cache
  exchanges are separate branch tests.
- No-event, no-matched-edge, and no-finite-response cases remain in coverage and
  fail the corresponding claim rather than disappearing.

## Required experiment gates

1. Freeze path selection and thresholds before held-out H/N analysis.
2. Test entity, negation, temporal, and scope pairs with paraphrase, rename,
   position, irrelevant-source, neighbor-carrier, equal-norm random,
   direct-only, V-only, K-only, and source-blind controls.
3. Report local-to-finite sign accuracy, rank correlation, and effect curves
   across intervention doses.
4. Require stability across tasks and generator sources on a source-balanced
   held-out natural-data bridge.
5. If teacher-forcing third-party generations, claim only the observer model's
   conditional computation.

Failure of semantic selectivity, numerical closure, finite-effect agreement, or
held-out stability falsifies the mechanism interpretation. Source allocation
remains a conditional ancestry diagnostic, not the main contribution.
